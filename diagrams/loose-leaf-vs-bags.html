How many cups the same scoop of tea yields

Whole leaf opens slowly and keeps going; milled leaf gives everything at once and is spent on the first cup.

Infusions from the same scoop

Whole leaf
1 2 3 3 infusions
Milled leaf
1     1 infusion · spent
The difference is exposed surface area. Milled leaf has all of its surface in contact with the water from the first second, so it releases everything together and has nothing left for later. Whole leaf rehydrates gradually and keeps giving flavour on the second and third cup.
