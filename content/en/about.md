## How I got into tea

I did not come to tea through family tradition or an early calling. I came to it for health reasons: {{< rawhtml >}}<mark>drinking tea started as something I had to do, not something I was looking for.</mark>{{< /rawhtml >}} What began as an obligation turned into something else as I started paying attention to what I was drinking.

And that is where the problem appeared: almost every tea I could find was bagged and milled, without nuance. When I first tried whole leaf, I understood I had been drinking a different beverage under the same name. That was the real starting point.

## Why LauTea exists

LauTea began in **2023**, in Quilmes, as a project to rediscover tea through **whole leaves** and **blends**. The idea is simple: whole leaf has an aromatic range that milled leaf cannot offer, and combining it with spices, flowers or fruit opens a spectrum a tea bag does not have.

Every blend we make serves a purpose and enhances a particular quality of the tea. It is not mixing for the sake of mixing: it is understanding what each ingredient contributes, and in what proportion.

## What I do today

I work on three fronts, and all three are part of the same thing:

- **Author blends** with whole leaves, designed for different moments of the day.
- **Writing and research**: I investigate and write about tea with sources, because there is a lot of unsupported information being repeated out there.
- **A community** — the one that grew on Instagram around all of this.

I write the articles on this site myself. If I cannot back something with a source, I say so; and if the evidence is weak, I make that clear in the text. It is the only way to talk about tea without repeating myths.
